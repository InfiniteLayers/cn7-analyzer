Pre-Flight Datalogging Guide

              
The 6 Golden Rules for capturing flawless calibration logs like a pro

            

          

          
        


        

          
          

            

              1
              Engine Warmup (Crucial Thermal Protection)
            

            

              Never floor a cold engine. Ensure Engine Coolant Temp (ECT) is at least 180°F (82°C) and engine oil is warm before entering full boost to prevent excessive piston ring and rod bearing wear.
            

          


          
          

            

              2
              Gear Selection (3rd or 4th Gear)
            

            

              Never log in 1st or 2nd gear — wheel spin and rapid RPM acceleration prevent the turbo wastegate controller from stabilizing. Use 3rd gear (manual transmission) or 4th gear in Manual Mode (DCT / automatic).
            

          


          
          

            

              

                3
                The ~3,000 RPM Sweet Spot (LSPI Prevention)
              

              SAFETY RULE
            

            

              Never floor the throttle below 2,500 RPM in high gears. Low-RPM high-boost load creates extreme cylinder pressure at slow piston speeds, causing LSPI (Low-Speed Pre-Ignition) and bent connecting rods. Stabilize speed at 2,800 to 3,000 RPM before stomping the throttle.
            

          


          
          

            

              4
              100% Flat-to-the-Floor Throttle
            

            

              Once you commit to the pull, hold the gas pedal 100% flat against the floorboard without fluttering or lifting. Partial throttle changes disrupt the ECU boost PID feedback loop and invalidate dyno measurements.
            

          


          
          

            

              5
              Full RPM Sweep (To ~6,500 RPM)
            

            

              Hold wide-open throttle through peak torque until roughly 6,500 RPM (or your vehicleâ€™s calibrated redline). Don't lift early at 5,000 RPM, or your tuner cannot assess high-RPM knock safety and fuel pump delivery.
            

          


          
          

            

              6
              Shift & Lift (Safe Cooldown)
            

            

              At redline, shift or lift off the throttle smoothly. Stop datalogging on your device, cruise gently for 1-2 minutes to allow turbo oil flow and intercooler recovery, then export your CSV.
